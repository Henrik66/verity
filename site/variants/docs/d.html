5.2Construction · conjunction

  1. Operands of p₁ and … and pₙ are evaluated left to right.
  2. If some pᵢ is a falsity, let k be the first such index: V = falsity, T = T(pₖ). Operands after pₖ are not evaluated.
  3. Otherwise V = verity and T = all(T(p₁), …, T(pₙ)). Nested all nodes from and are spliced in place. A because node is never flattened.

5.3Rendering

def valid_username(name) =
    (len(name) >= 3
        because f"{name!r} has at least 3 characters")
    and (len(name) <= 16
        because f"{name!r} has at most 16 characters")
    and (string_ops.is_alnum(name)
        because f"{name!r} is alphanumeric")
