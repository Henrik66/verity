Plate I · a falsity, explained

Every falsity comes pressed with its reasons.

Ask Verity whether "al" is a valid username. It doesn't just say no. It hands back a specimen of why, labeled down to the values it saw.

validate.ity
def valid_username(name) =
    (len(name) >= 3
        because f"{name!r} has at least 3 characters")
    and (len(name) <= 16
        because f"{name!r} has at most 16 characters")
    and (string_ops.is_alnum(name)
        because f"{name!r} is alphanumeric")

valid_username("al") |> explain |> print
