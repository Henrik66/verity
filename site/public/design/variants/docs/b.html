Testimony Semantics

The node kinds, how each operator constructs them, how testimony.explain renders them, and when the compiler may omit them.

5.1Node kinds

A testimony is a tree of nodes. The kinds are:

KindProduced byChildrenStatus
comparisona op bnonesettled
allverity from andone per conjunctsettled
nonefalsity from orone per disjunctsettled
notnot pT(p)proposed
becausep because mT(p), unless literalsettled
counterexamplefalsity from forallbody's refutationsettled
witnessverity from existsbody's witnesssettled

5.2Construction rules

Below, T(e) is the testimony of a truth e, and V(e) its value.

Conjunction

and.falsity
If some pᵢ is a falsity, let k be the first such index. Then V = falsity and T = T(pₖ). Operands after pₖ are not evaluated.
and.verity
Otherwise V = verity and T = all(T(p₁), …, T(pₙ)), with nested all nodes from and spliced in place.

5.3Rendering

explain(t) prints the value, then each node indented by two spaces per level.
