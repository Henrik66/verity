Appendix BGlossary

all_of
The accumulating conjunction: evaluates every operand and reports every failure. Section 5.2.
asserted
Said of a testimony node whose content is the author's claim (because, literal) rather than a computed fact. Section 5.1.
audience
Who a rendering is for: "developer" (the full tree) or "user" (claims only). Section 5.5.
because
Operator attaching a claim to a truth: p because m. Renders as witnessed: m or refuted: m. Section 4.5.
branched on
Said of a truth whose only use is as a condition. Its testimony is not built. Section 5.9.
conformance suite
The programs in examples/ and their expected outputs. They define testimony rendering. Section 11.7.
counterexample
The testimony node of a falsity produced by forall: the first element that refuted the body. Section 5.2.
diagnostic code
A stable identifier such as E0101 for a kind of compiler message. Section 10.3.
dual compilation
Compiling each truth-returning function into an evidence version and a value version, so that unobserved testimony costs nothing. Section 5.10.
falsity
The false constructor of truth. Its testimony is a refutation.
fix
A machine-applicable edit attached to a diagnostic. Section 10.2.
hint
Text attached to a reason that says how to make a refuted claim hold. Section 5.3.
lifting
Converting a Python bool into a truth with a lifted testimony node. Section 7.2.
observed
Said of testimony that is not only branched on. Observed testimony is built. Section 5.9.
reason
A claim with a stable dotted ID and an optional hint, used with because. Section 5.3.
record
One JSON Lines object written by verity with --json. Section 10.2.
refutation
The testimony of a falsity.
runtime
The Python modules testimony and string_ops, embedded in the compiler and published as verity-testimony. Section 11.6.
secret
A wrapped value that testimony shows as <secret>. Section 5.7.
testimony
The tree of evidence carried by every truth. Section 5.
TestimonyError
The AssertionError subclass raised by a failing assert, carrying the falsity. Section 3.6.
truth
Verity's type of truth values, with constructors verity and falsity. Section 3.2.
verity
The true constructor of truth. Its testimony is a witness.
witness
The testimony of a verity. Also the node kind produced by exists, naming the first element that satisfied the body.
